# Etmita

**IT-Agentur · B2B software, engineered in Germany**

Etmita UG (haftungsbeschränkt) designs, builds and operates B2B SaaS for regulated
European markets — with a security- and compliance-first engineering culture, end to
end: architecture, infrastructure, product and go-to-market.

## Products

### Passfest — Digital Product Passport
A compliance platform for the EU Ecodesign for Sustainable Products Regulation (ESPR)
and the EU Battery Regulation. Passfest structures product, material and supply-chain
data and generates a public, QR-addressable Digital Product Passport for every product —
built for the European Mittelstand.
→ [passfest.de](https://passfest.de) · [passfest.com](https://passfest.com)

### MitaBase — Dispatch-Software für Krankenfahrdienste
Dispatch and operations software for medical transport providers in Germany:
scheduling, coordination and fleet workflows in one platform.
→ [mitabase.com](https://mitabase.com) · [mitabase.de](https://mitabase.de)

## Team
A ~15-person product team across Back-End, Front-End, UI/UX, Marketing and Social
Media — designing, building and running our platforms end to end.

## How we build
- **DSGVO/GDPR-first** — German hosting, data minimization, encryption at rest, tested backups.
- **One stack, TypeScript throughout** — Next.js, NestJS microservices, PostgreSQL, containerized and continuously delivered.
- **Security and reliability as requirements**, not afterthoughts.
