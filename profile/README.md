# Etmita

> **Building compliant B2B software for Europe.**
>
> German software company focused on secure, scalable SaaS for regulated industries.

Etmita UG (haftungsbeschränkt) develops modern B2B platforms that help European businesses meet complex regulatory requirements without sacrificing usability or performance.

From product architecture and cloud infrastructure to design, engineering and go-to-market, everything is built in-house with a security-first mindset.

---

## Products

### 🚀 Passfest
**Digital Product Passport Platform**

Passfest enables manufacturers to comply with the EU Digital Product Passport ecosystem, including the **ESPR** and **EU Battery Regulation**.

It centralizes product, material and supply-chain data, generating secure, QR-addressable Digital Product Passports built for the European Mittelstand.

🌐 https://passfest.com  
🇩🇪 https://passfest.de

---

### 🚑 MitaBase
**Medical Transport Operations Platform**

Modern dispatch software for medical transport providers in Germany.

Scheduling, dispatching, fleet coordination, digital workflows and operations—all in one platform.

🌐 https://mitabase.com
